What kind of healthcare experience do you want to help create? Every interaction matters. Every voice matters. Every patient matters.

Patient Experience Excellence is not the responsibility of one person or department — it's a shared commitment to listening, respecting, communicating, involving, responding, and continuously learning from the people we serve. IPEE invites healthcare professionals, students, patients, advocates, researchers, organisations, and other health-sector stakeholders to make that commitment personal.

"Clinical excellence and human experience should go hand in hand."
